## Contract v1.1 (ADR-016-1) in one paragraph

v1 model rules (metres, `bakeAxisConversion`, normals imported, MikkTSpace tangents, no material/texture sub-assets, Read/Write off) plus: `animationType Humanoid` with the avatar created from **every** file (Unity's CopyFromOther also copies the source's mesh transforms and rejects files whose meshes differ — measured); the same explicit 52-bone human mapping written into every importer (`ArtCharacterContract.TemplateHumanBones`: Unity names for the 22 body bones, `<Side><Finger>1..3` → `<Side> <Finger> Proximal/Intermediate/Distal`, Pinky → Little); `optimizeGameObjects` off (bones stay Transforms for re-pointing, sockets, anchors); animation only from `Clips/`, every take looping with root rotation/height/XZ locked; no blend shapes. Textures: class **Character** (2048, streamed), roles `Mask` and `DetailMask` (linear, alpha kept). The gate (`ArtAssetGateTests`, `ArtCharacterTests`) verifies all of it, including that every avatar's human mapping equals its source's.

## How the characters run (WP-018: the Animator owns the pose)

The M1 primitive rigs stay in the hierarchy, hidden, only for their gameplay nodes: the three region hitboxes, the attack overlap socket and the armour roots are re-homed onto the bones / anchors at template build (`ZombieSkinnedVisual.RehomeGameplayNodes`: Head +0.10, Chest +0.02, Hips −0.40 m; `AttackSocket` on the Chest bone, 0.55 m forward — moved to the `Visual` node in WP-032, see below) — a bullet lands where the body is drawn and the strike tests where the animated chest is. `ProceduralPoseBridge` is gone (WP-018); nothing writes bone rotations from the block rig any more.

**Zombie.** `ZombieAnimator` keeps its M1 API (`SetLocomotion / SetAttack / AddFlinch / BeginStagger / BeginDeath / Tick / Pose / IsStaggering / DeathFinished`) and forwards to the `IZombieAnimationDriver` = `ZombieSkinnedVisual` when the set is authored: gait = shamble A/B/C by the look's fit, run for Runners, rate `GaitRate` = ground speed ÷ clip `speed_mps` (eased back to 1 at rest so the idle does not crawl); attacks = the clip of the tier's style (Overhead → sweep) at `AttackRate` = clip `hit_window_start` ÷ tier `TelegraphTime`, so the window opens exactly at the config telegraph (swipe ×0.97, lunge ×0.95, sweep ×1.00); flinch = regional additive (head/torso/leg) + `Hit_L/R` on the upper-body override when the impact ≥ 0.9; a gameplay stagger (REQ-124 window) shows as the upper-body hit while locomotion is suppressed by the enemy; the full-body `hit_heavy_stagger` is reserved for an armour break (`ArmorVisualBinder` → `NotifyArmorBroken`); death = one of the four clips from the M1 style (fwd / back / SpinFall → side / Crumple & HeadshotDrop → kneel), `DeathFinished` on the clip's `death_end` event (2 s timer as a safety net); `rise` on spawn behind `ZombieEnemy.Rise` (WP-040, DEC-081: **hybrid** — only a spawn the director judged off camera rises; `RiseOnSpawn` is the Always/Off probe view; the body neither walks nor swings until `rise_end`; the WP-040 beat layer raises `spawn_rise` / `spawn_land` around it). **Ultra attack clip (WP-040):** `TierBehaviourProfile.AttackClip = AP009_zombie_attack_overhead` (AP-009B v002, `asset-production/AP-009/exports/v002`) is the version's own clip; until WP-032 (S-06) imports the v002 set and regenerates `DD_Zombie.controller` with a fourth attack index, `ZombieSkinnedVisual.PlayAttack` keeps playing the sweep placeholder for the Overhead style (`AttackClipPlaceholder`); the switch is data (`ResolvedAttackClip`), no enemy code changes when the clip lands. `hit_heavy_stagger` (armour break) now holds the enemy until the clip's `stagger_end` (`ZombieSkinnedVisual.IsHeavyStaggering`, 1.4 s cap).

**DEC-029 through the clip (ADR-018-1).** `EnemyAttackState` is the `IAttackWindowSink`: with a driven body the Windup→Active edge is the clip's `hit_window_start` `AnimationEvent` and Active→Recovery is `hit_window_end` — the hitbox is live exactly while the strike is drawn; the code timers stay as a 0.5 s safety net (`WindowFallbackCount`, expected 0) and as the whole machine on the block rig. `TryConnect`, damage, cooldown and recovery are untouched.

**Player.** `PlayerProceduralAnimator` keeps its hooks (`PlayFireRecoil / PlayDamageFlinch / PlayDeath / PlayReload`) and forwards to `PlayerSkinnedVisual` (no writes to the block rig): locomotion = character-space velocity ÷ 6.2 m/s on `MoveX`/`MoveY` with `LocoRate` (idle-safe easing); `Fire` additive, `Hit` override (movement, aim and fire untouched — REQ-044/045), `Pickup` on `WeaponEvents.SpecialWeaponPickedUp` (no movement lock), death fwd/back from the last hit's push. `PlayerAimLayer` (IK pass of `aim_layer`) turns Spine/Chest/UpperChest toward `PlayerAim.AimDirection` (±35° yaw split over three bones, ±20° pitch) on top of the base — the root still turns toward the mouse in code (REQ-038). The weapon mount sits on `socket_weapon_r`; the AP-009 clips hold the weapon at 1.37 m (C09), which closes the WP-016 "low weapon" note.

**Events (ADR-018-1).** `CharacterAnimationEvents` sits next to each Animator and implements the manifest names as methods (`fire`, `hit_window_start/end`, `foot_l/r`, `death_end`, `rise_end`, `pickup`, `breath`, `hit`, `stagger_end`); owners subscribe to the instance `Raised`, effects (S-07) and audio (S-08) to the static `Any`. Zero allocation per event.

**Crowd budget (ADR-018-2).** Zombie Animators cull with `CullUpdateTransforms` (state machine and events keep running off screen; WP-032 switched the zombie prefab to `AlwaysAnimate` — see below); `CharacterAnimatorLod` steps a body by hand at 15 Hz once its LODGroup would show LOD2+ (same screen-height rule, `lodBias` included) — events still fire, the clock never stops. The 40-body animation path allocates 0 B per frame (`AnimationTests.Kalabalik40…`).

## Polish & readability (WP-032, ADR-032-1)

Built-in Humanoid IK only (no Animation Rigging). **Look-at:** `CharacterLookAt` next to each Animator (`SetLookAtWeight/Position` in `OnAnimatorIK`, once per frame) — the player looks at the S-02 aim point (head 0.6 / body 0.05, single source `PlayerAim`), the zombie at `EnemyTargeting.Player` + 1.5 m (0.8 / 0.15); weight fades to 0 beyond 80° (25° fade), off while dying / rising / waiting / far LOD. **Foot IK:** `FootIkAdapter` (base layer `iKPass`, every locomotion state `iKOnFeet`) rays 0.5 m up / 0.6 m down on the aim-block mask, foot target = hit + `leftFeetBottomHeight`, ≤ 0.35 m correction, pelvis follows via `bodyPosition`; LOD0/1 only and at most 12 bodies per frame (`MaxBodiesPerFrame`). **Stride matching:** `StrideCalibrator` samples every locomotion clip on the Unity-retargeted rig at build time (planted foot from the `foot_l/r` events, travel axis per clip) and writes `unitySpeedMps` into the animation set; `GaitRate`/`LocoRate` divide by the calibrated speed (`CharacterAnimationSet.SpeedOf`), manifest `speed_mps` is the fallback. Measured slip on the retargeted rig ≤ 1 cm/step (test threshold 2 cm, `AnimationPolishTests.Stride…`). **Telegraph bridge:** `DD_Zombie` has an additive upper-body `telegraph` layer with three generated clips (`Zombie/Generated/DD_Telegraph_{Swipe,Lunge,Sweep}.anim`, muscle curves, additive reference = frame 0) played at `TelegraphRate = 1 / telegraphSeconds`; the layer weight is the tier emphasis `1 / TierBehaviourProfile.TelegraphScale` clamped to 0..1 (a shortened wind-up gets the full emphasis, a lengthened one proportionally less) — the `hit_window_start/end` events and the attack clip's timing are untouched (DEC-029 unchanged, tested). **Wait state:** `ZombieAnimator.SetWaiting(bool)` (S-03 crowd/queue hook) moves the body into the `Wait` state (WP-032B: the v002 `idle_queue` clip with a per-body `WaitPhase` cycle offset — a queue never shifts its weight in unison; events `shift_l/shift_r` for S-07/S-08) and drops the look-at. **Rise:** `ZombieEnemy.RiseOnSpawn` stays a probe switch; the shipped policy is DEC-081 hybrid (S-03 WP-040: only an off-screen spawn rises; the clip's `rise_end` ends the handshake, `spawn_rise/spawn_land` beats on `EnemyCombatEvents`).

## Zombie look (ADR-016-2)

`ZombieLookSelector.Select(library, spawnSeed)` — pure, deterministic: fit A/B/C, top, bottom, head (H0 bald = no mesh) and colourways from a xorshift stream seeded by the EnemyFactory spawn seed. ## Armour states (ADR-017-1, WP-017)

`ArmorVisualBinder` (plain class owned by `ZombieAppearance` when `DD_Characters.ZombieArmor` is set and the prefab has armour slots) implements the WP-006 `IArmorVisual` contract on real meshes: Absent → renderers off; Present → the variant's Present main + sub (kabuto | dō + kote | haidate + suneate); Damaged → the Damaged main (one panel / lame / tasset gone, flaked lacquer), same sub; Broken → the family's remnants (cords / straps, ≤ 400 tris), never nothing, region open. Idempotent, allocation-free after construction; the break raises `ArmorVisualBinder.Broken` / `AnyBroken` with slot, region, the AP-007 anchor and the variant — the S-07 effect hook (no effect yet). The M1 plate placeholders are never shown on an armoured skinned body. Variants per slot come from the spawn seed (`ArmorLookSelector`, hashed — the LCG spawn seeds gave 2 variant sets in 40 through a linear pick; measured), equally weighted; whether a slot shows anything is still the gameplay `ArmorSet` roll (WP-006/010 untouched). The garment draw is re-run under the equipped pieces' constraints (`ZombieLookConstraints`: AP-008 exclusions — no T7 under a dō, T2 not under D1, T6 not under any dō; only B2/B5/B7 under a haidate; K2 only on H0/H1 — and the readability rule: faded cw2 cloth under a Present/Damaged Body or Leg), once per fitted slot, never relaxed within a life (a Broken plate keeps its cloth). The unconstrained draw is byte-for-byte the WP-016 look (same xorshift stream). Tests: `ArtArmorTests` (EditMode: contract, manifest naming and budgets, library, exclusions, 8 000 constrained draws with 0 forbidden combinations), `ArmorTests` (PlayMode: 40 armoured zombies — 5 renderers each, 1 material, 8 variant sets, rules met; Present → Damaged → Broken through real hits with the event on `anchor_helmet`; DEC-077 armour reading).
