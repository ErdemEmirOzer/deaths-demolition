## LUT contract (ADR-029-1)

The user LUT runs in URP's LDR grading after the internal grade: the shader converts the graded colour to sRGB, samples the strip with it and takes the texel as the sRGB-coded result (`Colors.hlsl ApplyColorGrading`). So a strip that Lightroom processed with the recipe reproduces the recipe exactly — provided the texture imports **linear** (sRGB off), uncompressed RGBA32, no mips, bilinear, clamp (`ArtImportContract.ApplyLut`, applied by the postprocessor to everything under `LUT/`; `LookLightingTests` checks it). The recipe is applied to the reference frames rendered **with** the WP-013 grade, so the LUT sits on top of that grade and nothing is applied twice. Only global tone/colour operations survive the strip: `Adaptive:*` (masks), `Grain-*`, `Sharpening-*`, `Vignetting-*`, `Optics-*`, `Portraits-*` presets and any local tool are not part of a recipe (vignette stays a URP override). `LutBuilder.Measure` applies the strip on the CPU the way the shader does and scores it against the Lightroom frame in CIE76 ΔE (`-executeMethod …LutBuilder.MeasureCli -ddLut -ddSource -ddTarget [-ddOut]`).

Reference frames: `LookReferenceTests` (PlayMode) renders fifteen 1080p frames (WP-075 §6.2; eight until WP-075 C2) of Run + World_AP005 with the real characters (5 region gameplay framings, the Presentation A overview, the 40 crowd, a player close-up, `center-shadow`, three third-person and three first-person frames) at fixed cloud-shadow phases (the centre in a sun patch; `center-shadow` in the cloud shadow; phases in the manifest), each `raw` (no post) and `post` (the gameplay grade), to `DD_ART_CAPTURE_DIR/look-ref/`.

## Cloud shadow (WP-075 C2)

The sun carries a runtime cookie (`CloudShadowDriver`, added by `WorldAtmosphere` in play mode only — the lightmapper never sees it, so moving clouds are not baked into the indirect light): AP-023's `T_cloud_cookie` v002 (`Resources/DD_CloudCookie.png`, linear single-channel BC4, Repeat, no mip streaming — `ArtImportContract.ApplyLightCookie`), 240 × 240 m on the ground, drifting 1.3 m/s toward 52°. URP projects a directional cookie in light space, so the driver sets size.y = 240 · sin(elevation) and publishes the same mapping for custom shaders as two rows (`_DD_CloudCookieU/V`, `Shaders/DD_CloudCookie.hlsl`: `DD_CloudCookieSample`, `DD_CloudSunLit01`) plus `_DD_ShaftDir`. World shaders that shade the main light themselves (`DD_GroundBlend`, `DD_Foliage_Wind`, `DD_Water_v2`, `DD_TierLit`) compile `_LIGHT_COOKIES`. `CloudShadowDriver.ForcedPhase` / env `DD_CLOUD_PHASE` fixes the drift phase for captures and the DEC-077 four-phase runs.

## Bake policy (ADR-029-2)

`ArtLightingBake.PrepareWorld` (part of `Run Look`) writes into `World_AP005.unity`: a **Mixed** directional sun with `WorldAtmosphere`'s parameters (the runtime finds it by name — `WorldAtmosphere.FindBakedSun` — and does not build a second one), the chart policy below on every static instance (`PlacementImporter` applies the same policy on re-import) and `ContributeGI` on the stand-in ground, a `LightProbeGroup` (route samples every 4 m, spawn / weapon points, a 6 m grid over ±48 m; heights 0.6 / 2.0 m, grid also 4.5 m), six baked reflection probes (one per region + the pond), the lighting settings asset, and the runtime environment (ambient trilight, sky, fog) so the bake sees the runtime sky. `tools/bake-lighting.sh` runs `ArtLightingBake.BakeWorld` in batch **with** a graphics device (Progressive GPU): re-import stale contract models → prepare → `Lightmapping.Bake()` → save → report. Lightmaps land in `Scenes/World_AP005/` (EXR, LFS). Budget: lightmaps ≤ 64 MB.

**Who gets a lightmap chart** (`ArtLightingBake.ApplyLightmapLodPolicy`): structures and ground — and of those only the **LOD0** renderers; LOD1+ are probe-lit and do not contribute (bake3 listed 1096 overlapping charts = 328 instances × 3 LOD levels, two thirds of them the tiny LOD1/LOD2 charts). WP-038: the AP-015P ground / route / paving plates contribute to the bake (occluders, bounce off the ground) and stay probe-lit (no lightmap UVs — flat, huge charts would buy nothing; `ArtImportContract.IsSurfaceModel`); water does not contribute. Dressing (lanterns, rocks, benches, props) stays static but probe-lit throughout (small charts, 1534 overlaps at the first bake). Vegetation and breakables are not static. Environment models import with generated lightmap UVs (Contract v1.2: `generateSecondaryUV`, hard angle 88°, angle/area distortion 8/15) and the chart margin is **calculated for half the bake resolution** (`secondaryUVMarginMethod = Calculate`, min lightmap resolution 8 texels/m = ½ × `ArtLightingBake.LightmapResolution`, min object scale 1). The "min lightmap resolution" is the lowest resolution the margin must survive — a smaller value means a wider margin: Unity's default 40 gave 1096 overlapping charts at 16 texels/m, 16 still left 122 (railings / posts / panels), 32 shrank the margin to 299, 8 leaves 78 (world v2) / 82 (world v3) — thin railing / post / panel charts; accepted. `ArtLightingBake.ReportChartsCli` lists every charted renderer with its estimated and baked footprint (the surface-area estimate is within ~10 % of the atlas footprint Unity assigns). Characters stay UV2-less (skinned, probe-lit).

Renderer (`ArtLightingBake.ConfigureRenderer`): the SSAO feature at its low-cost setting (downsample, after opaque, depth-normals source, low normal quality, sample count Low, medium blur, intensity 1.2, radius 0.30 m, direct lighting strength 0.25, falloff 80 m) and main-light shadows 60 m / 4 cascades (was 45). MSAA **4× → 2×** on the pipeline asset and the Ultra quality level (ADR-025-2 measurement, WP-025: render-target driver memory 413.6 → 334.3 MB at 2×; C-00's technical decision applied here; acceptance = RT ≤ 340 MB, no DEC-077 change, edge quality accepted by the AD in the A/B); HDR and render scale 1.0 stay, no FXAA/SMAA. `-executeMethod …ArtLightingBake.SetSsaoCli -ddSsao off|on` toggles the feature for A/B captures.
