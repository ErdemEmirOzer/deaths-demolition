Modified for Deaths Demolition in 2026 (decision DEC-109, WP-068), so the game's Turkish headings draw in this face:
- Added Ğ (U+011E), ğ (U+011F), İ (U+0130), Ş (U+015E) and ş (U+015F).
  - Ğ ğ: a newly drawn breve (two unencoded marks, brevecomb.case.dd and brevecomb.dd) over the face's own G and g.
  - İ: the face's own Ü dot over I.
  - Ş ş: the face's own combining cedilla (U+0327) under S and s, placed the way the face places it in Ç and ç.
  The new glyphs are composites of the original outlines plus these marks; no original glyph was changed.
- Font names: family "Shippori Mincho B1 DD-TR", PostScript name "ShipporiMinchoB1DDTR-Bold". The copyright
  string is kept and one sentence about this modification is appended to it.
- The OS/2 code page 1254 (Turkish) and Latin Extended-A bits are set. The DSIG table is removed because it no
  longer matches the file.
